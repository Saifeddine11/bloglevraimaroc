Quick answer: Timitar 2026 runs in Agadir from 23 to 25 July 2026. Maroc.ma/MAP reported on 17 July 2026 that the theme is “Amazigh artists welcome the music of the world”. The named venues are Place Al Amal and Théâtre de Verdure.

![Large Moroccan square, useful to understand festival flows.](/images/articles/tourisme-prix-immobiliers-marrakech.webp)

## What to retain

The 16 July 2026 MAP note lists artists including Lartiste, Benny Adam, Najat Atabou, Malouma, Fatima Tihihite, Larbi Imghrane, Zouhair Bahaoui, Hamid El Kasri, Ragheb Alama, Oudaden, Abeer Nehme and Fatima Tabaamrant.

![Moroccan riad, a lodging type often sought during events.](/images/articles/riads-marrakech-rendement-pieges.webp)

## Why it matters

For Agadir in late July 2026, the festival adds a cultural reason to stay in the city beyond the beach, supporting nights, restaurants and local transport.

![Marrakech medina as an image of Moroccan urban culture.](/images/articles/tourisme-maroc-2030-medina-2026.webp)

## Watch points

Because the festival is free, arrive early, check the daily programme, carry water and plan the return before concerts end.
