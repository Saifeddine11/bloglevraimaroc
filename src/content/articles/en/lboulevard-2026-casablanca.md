## Day-by-day programme

| Date             | Main stage                                                                    | Key point                     |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Thu 17 September | Dizzy Dros, Figoshin, BO9AL, DJ Fly                                           | Rap and hip-hop, from 4 pm    |
| Fri 18 September | Rhita Nattah, Bab L’Bluz, Issam Kamal & Mazagan                               | Fusion and Moroccan rock      |
| Sat 19 September | Sepultura, Sotherion, Suicide Machine, Mycose Of You                          | Rock and metal night          |
| Sun 20 September | Tremplin winners, Zentone, Sarāb, Kin’Gongolo Kiniata, Omar EK, Aziz Konkrite | Closing night and discoveries |

Access is announced as free. The venue is the R.U.C stadium in Casablanca, with opening from 4 pm according to official and partner communications.

## Tremplin, Souk and practical notes

The Tremplin remains the festival’s discovery engine. The official Souk is scheduled from 17 to 20 September from 4 pm with more than 30 stands. Tremplin training sessions are scheduled from 21 to 26 September at R.U.C, Boultek and Technopark.

## What to check before going

Check the official page on the day for running order, entry rules, prohibited items and weather changes. If you are coming from Marrakech, plan your return carefully because late festival hours do not always align with intercity transport.
