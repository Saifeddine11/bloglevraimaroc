Substantive editorial update: **30 October 2026**. Original publication date retained: 16 September 2026.

**The Gentlemen Season 3 is officially in production in Morocco and the United Kingdom.** Netflix confirmed that on 23 October 2026. In Morocco, Le360 reports that filming started in Rabat on 13 September and names Erfoud as another stop. Netflix has not published a detailed Morocco schedule, set addresses or a release date.

## The answer in 30 seconds

- **Official status:** Season 3 is filming in the UK and Morocco, according to Netflix Tudum.
- **Reported Morocco locations:** Rabat and Erfoud, according to Le360; Netflix has not published set addresses.
- **Cast confirmed on 23 October:** Theo James and Kaya Scodelario, joined by names including Robert Carlyle, Elijah Wood and Orlando Bloom.
- **Production:** Moonage Pictures is publicly associated with the series; the dedicated legal entity is **MOONAGE PICTURES (THE GENTLEMEN) LIMITED**, UK company 14115797.
- **Vehicles in Morocco:** Accrocar tells Le Vrai Maroc that it supplied a Rolls-Royce Cullinan and Bentley Bentayga for the Morocco operation entrusted to it.
- **Essential limit:** supplying a production does not prove that a vehicle will appear in the final edit.
- **Release:** no official date or window as of 30 October 2026.

## What Netflix has officially confirmed

On 23 October 2026, Netflix Tudum announced that the third season was **currently filming in the United Kingdom and Morocco**. It is the latest official source on production status. The update also confirms Theo James as Eddie Horniman and Kaya Scodelario as Susie Glass.

Netflix describes a new phase in Eddie’s ambition but does not publish a map of the Morocco shoot. The word “Morocco” should not be expanded into an invented list of scenes, hotels or addresses. Netflix has also announced no release date.

Guy Ritchie remains the creator and director associated with the series. Season 3 continues the crime world launched in 2024, but filming in Morocco does not by itself prove that Rabat or Erfoud will be named as such on screen.

## Morocco filming: what is established about Rabat and Erfoud

Le360 reported on 14 September 2026 that filming had started the previous day, Sunday 13 September, in **Rabat**. The publication added, attributing the information to a source, that **Erfoud** had also been selected for scenes.

The capital provides an urban, institutional and Atlantic environment; Erfoud opens onto pre-Saharan landscapes and more demanding travel distances. That explains their potential production value, but it is not a description of filmed scenes.

## Production companies and Morocco’s production ecosystem

ITV Studios identifies **Moonage Pictures** as the producer of *The Gentlemen*. UK Companies House separately lists **MOONAGE PICTURES (THE GENTLEMEN) LIMITED**, active company 14115797, whose stated business is television programme production. Both records are public and verifiable.

[Dune Films](https://dunefilms.com/) presents itself as a Moroccan production-services company. Its public site documents experience across major international shoots, but its current credits page does not list *The Gentlemen*. Dune Films’ specific involvement in the operation described here is therefore attributed to statements and first-party material supplied by Accrocar to Le Vrai Maroc, not to a Netflix announcement.

This separation matters: Netflix confirms the series and filming countries; Le360 reports Rabat and Erfoud; the businesses describe their own services. One source does not replace another.

Morocco offers more than landscapes. An international production needs production services, technical crews, transport, security, accommodation, workshops and specialist suppliers. Rabat tests urban coordination; Erfoud requires greater autonomy and control over distance.

The vehicle example gives that chain a concrete shape. A luxury car intended for production must be prepared, documented, transported and available on time without exposing the schedule or the people involved. That operational context is the original reporting advantage of this briefing.
