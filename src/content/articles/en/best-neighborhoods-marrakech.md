## How to read Marrakech neighborhoods

Marrakech does not have a single center. It works through uses: Medina for history, Guéliz for the modern city, Hivernage for hotel prestige, Agdal for modern residences, Targa for family living, Palmeraie for villas, Sidi Ghanem for creative and industrial activity, and peripheral roads for leisure or expansion projects.

## The twelve neighborhoods

**Guéliz** is the most readable modern district: services, cafés, banks, restaurants and apartment buildings. It suits active residents, expatriates, MRE buyers and investors who want a liquid asset.

**Hivernage** is quieter and more premium, driven by hotels, Mohammed VI Avenue and high-end residences. It suits patrimonial buyers and premium furnished rental.

**Medina** is unique: alleys, souks, riads, patios and terraces. It suits experienced buyers and tourism projects, but access, works and legal checks are essential.

**Agdal** offers modern residences and a good compromise for families, MRE buyers and furnished rental. **Targa** is quieter and more family-oriented. **Palmeraie** is about villas, gardens and privacy, with higher maintenance costs. **Route de Casablanca**, **Route de l’Ourika**, **Chrifia**, **Mhamid**, **Sidi Ghanem** and **Bab Atlas** each require street-by-street reading.
