Buying a plot and building appeals to those who find ready-made villas too expensive or poorly finished. But the final budget depends on dozens of items that listings never show. Here is what the sources available in October 2026 say, including where they disagree.

## How much does a built square metre cost?

Published ranges vary widely, because they come from professionals who do not count the same things. All of them exclude the land.

- **Armonia Solutions** (updated June 2026): 5,500 to 8,000 MAD/m² for a standard build in Marrakech.
- **AE Architectes** (May 2026): 6,000 to 9,000 MAD/m² mid-range, 9,000 to 14,000 high-end, 14,000 to 20,000 and above for top-end, excluding pool and outdoor works.
- **Wall Constructions** (2026, VAT included, indicative): 3,200 to 4,400 MAD/m² for the complete structural shell, and roughly 8,500 to 10,000 turnkey high-end.

These are builders' and architects' price lists, not official statistics. As an order of magnitude for a 300 m² standard-finish project (simple arithmetic on the ranges above): 7,000 to 9,000 MAD/m² gives 2.1 to 2.7 million MAD, before land, pool and contingencies.

> Always compare two quotes on the same scope: Wall Constructions warns that some low quotes leave out the boundary wall or waterproofing.

## The overruns that derail budgets

![Villa with pool and wooded garden in Marrakech, where pool and outdoor works weigh on the budget.](/images/articles/construire-villa-marrakech-cout-permis-risques-2026-2.webp)

According to AE Architectes, a sloping plot adds 200,000 to 500,000 MAD (earthworks, special foundations), a basement or half-level 20 to 30% more, and utility connections 30,000 to 100,000 MAD. The firm recommends a 10% contingency, and Armonia at least as much, plus 15 to 20% of soft costs (permits, architect, taxes) on the whole.

Architect fees diverge: Armonia gives 3 to 5% of works cost, while AE Architectes quotes 280,000 to 420,000 MAD for a full mission on 400 m², clearly more than 3 to 5% (about 6 to 9% on 11,500 MAD/m², our own estimate). Ask exactly what the mission covers (design only, or site supervision included).

On timing, Armonia counts 8 to 12 months for a standard family home, Wall Constructions 12 to 18 months for a typical villa. AE Architectes claims costs rose 35 to 50% between 2020 and 2026, without backing the figure: a 2022 quote is no longer reliable.

## The building permit: steps and documents

![Plans and land documents, part of the building permit file.](/images/articles/construire-villa-marrakech-cout-permis-risques-2026-3.webp)

The permit is issued by the president of the communal council, and filing is done online through the Rokhas platform, according to Médias24. It is valid for one year. For an individual villa, review usually takes 30 days, longer if the project is complex.

The file includes the ownership certificate, cadastral plan, the urban agency's information note, signed plans, a copy of the national ID, the "ne varietur" undertaking and the architect's contract. Permit taxes are generally 20 to 30 MAD/m² of floor area, and the owner must be up to date on taxes, including the tax on undeveloped land (see our article on the [tax on undeveloped plots](/articles/land-tax-undeveloped-plots-marrakech-2026-law-14-25)).

Armonia adds that plans must be drawn by an architect registered with the Order, with an engineering office and, for most homes, a technical control office, in line with the RPS 2000 seismic standard. Wall Constructions mentions a mandatory architect from 150 m² built: check this threshold with the commune. Above all, make sure the land is buildable (see [buildable land in Marrakech](/articles/buildable-land-marrakech-zoning-note-permit-checks)).

## Building without a permit: the real risk

Starting works before authorisation exposes you to penalties. According to Médias24, commenting on law 66-12 on the control of urban planning offences, fines can reach 5 million MAD and prison terms 5 years depending on the offence, with inspectors holding judicial police status. Armonia adds that a house built without proper authorisations may be refused utility connection, sale or insurance. After completion, the occupancy permit is essential (see [occupancy permit and compliance certificate](/articles/occupancy-permit-compliance-certificate-buying-marrakech)).

## Ten-year liability and site supervision

Reaconsult notes that builders' liability runs ten years from handover of the works for defects affecting structural soundness or making the building unfit for use. This source does not say whether insurance is mandatory: have the contract specify it.

If a defect appears, the recommended approach is to have an independent expert record it before repair, check the reservations in the handover minutes, then send a formal notice. A technical control office or supervising architect, a soil study before sizing foundations and staged written payments reduce risk.

## Checklist before launching the project

- Check the land title, absence of mortgage and buildability (information note).
- Commission a soil study and topographic survey before fixing the budget.
- Get at least three quotes on the same specification, boundary walls, waterproofing and pool included.
- Allow 10% contingency and 15 to 20% soft costs, plus extra for slope or basement.
- File the permit through Rokhas and start only with the authorisation in hand.
- Write payment milestones, deadlines, penalties and insurance into the contract.
- Require the occupancy permit before treating the project as finished.

These figures are orders of magnitude from professionals and media; they do not replace a detailed quote or legal advice from a notary or lawyer.
