## Marrakech, Fez and Meknes: inland heat

Marrakech has a forecast maximum of 42°C, compared with 41°C in Fez and Meknes. Their minima range from 24°C to 27°C. An early outing and a long mid-afternoon walk involve different exposure.

Oujda’s forecast high is 36°C. A city value does not describe every municipality in a province: altitude, coastal proximity and local exposure can change conditions.

## Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier and Agadir

Forecast highs are 27°C in Casablanca, 37°C in Agadir, 30°C in Rabat and 33°C in Tangier. DGM also mentions low cloud, mist or drizzle near coasts and fairly strong gusts in the southeast and southern provinces.

A moderate air temperature does not establish calm seas. For a crossing or water activity, check maritime notices and the operator’s information separately.

## Orange warnings: where and when?

The DGM alert relayed on 7 September at 1.40 pm announces thunderstorms with hail and gusts, bringing 25–35 mm in the eighteen provinces listed in the article this Monday from 2 pm to 11 pm.

The heat warning includes Tata, Es-Semara, Assa-Zag, Boujdour, Oued Ed-Dahab and Aousserd, with 42–46°C from 7–9 September. Other provinces are covered on different dates; see our [heat and warning tracker](/en/articles/morocco-heatwave-august-2026-cities-forecast) for the list.

Always consult DGM’s warning map before departure. A provincial warning does not mean that every neighbourhood will experience identical weather at the same time.

## Dakhla and Laayoune: match the geographical area

Dakhla’s forecast is 17–28°C and Laayoune’s 22–42°C on 7 September. A city value does not describe a whole province or multiple days. Compare the same place and validity period.
