Quick answer: Maroc Météo reported on 2 July 2026 that temperatures were 5 to 12°C above seasonal normals. On 1 July 2026, more than nine cities exceeded 40°C, including 42.2°C in Kenitra and 40.0°C in Salé.

![Marrakech avenue, better early or late in the day.](/images/articles/gueliz-hivernage-palmeraie-investissement.webp)

## What to retain

DGM links the episode to the Chergui, a hot dry continental wind, and a Saharan thermal low. It also mentioned possible sand lifts and local thunderstorms over mountain areas.

![Agadir beach, often a more temperate option depending on the day.](/images/articles/tourisme-maroc-2030-agadir-2026.webp)

## Why it matters

Travellers should adapt timing: early visits, indoor breaks in the afternoon, air-conditioned long drives and extra care for children, older travellers and people with chronic illness.

![Moroccan riad, useful for shaded breaks.](/images/articles/riads-marrakech-rendement-pieges.webp)

## Watch points

Early July 2026 figures do not replace today’s warning. Check the official Maroc Météo bulletin before moving.
