Quick answer: the Kenitra-Marrakech axis sits at the centre of ONCF’s rail programme. On 24 April 2026, ONCF referred to a 96 billion dirham programme, including 53 billion for the new high-speed line. On 9 July 2026, AfDB announced €205 million for PADIF.

![Marrakech airport, complementing rail-air connectivity.](/images/articles/marrakech-2030-infrastructures-tourisme.webp)

## What to retain

ONCF describes a 430 km axis. As of 24 April 2026, land acquisition was completed, most rights of way were cleared and station works were expected by July 2026.

![Development area around Marrakech.](/images/articles/zones-amizmiz-agdal-targa.webp)

## Why it matters

For travellers, the issue is smoother links between Casablanca, Rabat, Kenitra and Marrakech. For tourism, multi-city stays become more realistic.

![Tanger Med, reminder of logistics corridors.](/images/articles/hcp-croissance-logistique-tanger-med-2026.webp)

## Watch points

Service dates, fares, frequencies and urban connections will matter more than investment totals. No cited source confirms commercial operation in July 2026.
