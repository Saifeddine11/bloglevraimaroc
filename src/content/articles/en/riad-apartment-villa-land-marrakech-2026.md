A riad to renovate in the medina currently trades around 8,000 to 14,000 MAD/m², while a villa with a pool in the Palmeraie reaches 22,000 to 38,000 MAD/m². For the same budget, these two properties suit neither the same investor profile nor the same level of legal risk or management burden. In Marrakech, the choice of property type often matters more than the choice of neighborhood for the final outcome. Here is what the 2026 figures say by category, and the points to watch for each.

## Four markets, four pricing logics

Riads, apartments, villas and land do not answer the same demand, nor share the same liquidity. The table below summarizes the ranges observed by neighborhood and property type in 2026:

| Property type | Area | Indicative price |
|---|---|---|
| Renovated prestige riad | Bab Doukkala, Mouassine, Riad Laarous | 25,000 – 40,000 MAD/m² |
| Riad to renovate | Derb Dabbachi, Riad Zitoun, Sidi Ben Slimane | 8,000 – 14,000 MAD/m² |
| Premium villa | Palmeraie, golf estates (Amelkis, Royal Palm) | 22,000 – 38,000 MAD/m² |
| Apartment | Hivernage | 18,000 – 26,000 MAD/m² |
| Apartment | Guéliz | 14,000 – 22,000 MAD/m² |
| Apartment | Agdal / Issil | 11,000 – 17,000 MAD/m² |
| Villa / land | Route de l'Ourika | 9,000 – 16,000 MAD/m² |
| Undeveloped land | Outskirts (Route de l'Ourika, Route de Fès) | 800 – 3,000 MAD/m² |

These orders of magnitude, established by RICS-accredited firm ReaConsult based on observed transactions and ANCFCC/DGI references, remain market averages to confirm property by property. The 2026 trend is judged stronger for renovated riads and Palmeraie villas (+3 to 6% according to industry professionals), driven by European buyers and anticipation linked to the 2030 World Cup. ## Riad in the medina: the highest yield, the most technical risk

![Traditional courtyard of a riad in the Marrakech medina with zellige tilework](/images/articles/riad-appartement-villa-terrain-marrakech-2026-2.webp)

On paper, the riad remains the most profitable property type in Marrakech: long-term rental yield of around 4.5% for a renovated prestige riad, up to 6.5% for a riad renovated and then rented long-term, and a gross short-term (guesthouse) yield that can reach 9 to 14% depending on standing and occupancy rate.

It is also the most technical asset. The land title area often includes patios and outbuildings: a riad advertised at 280 m² may offer only 180 m² of actual livable space, which is why an independent measurement before making an offer matters. Part of the medina's built stock remains under moulkiya status (unregistered customary title), which complicates bank financing and justifies a discount. Operating a riad as a guesthouse also requires a valid tourism license — a point many listings gloss over. See our [guide to land titles and moulkiya in Marrakech](/articles/titre-foncier-moulkiya-marrakech) and our [feature on renovating a riad in the medina](/articles/renover-riad-medina-marrakech-2026) for details.

## Apartment: the simplest compromise to secure

The apartment offers a markedly simpler legal framework: an individualized land title in most cases, classic co-ownership governed by law no. 18-00, and more accessible bank financing. Prices range from 11,000 MAD/m² in Agdal to 26,000 MAD/m² in Hivernage, for a long-term rental yield generally between 5% and 5.8% — more stable than a riad, even if it caps lower.

> An advertised yield is never a collected yield: between the announced gross figure and the real net return sit rental vacancy, co-ownership charges, taxation and, for short-term rentals, platform commissions.

For an off-plan purchase (VEFA), the law requires a payment schedule tied to actual construction progress and a mandatory escrow account from the reservation stage — a point too often overlooked by buyers in a hurry. See our [guide to VEFA in Morocco](/articles/vefa-maroc-echeancier-paiement-garanties-legales) and our [feature on new apartments in Marrakech](/articles/appartement-neuf-marrakech-2026) for details.

## Villa: standing, running costs and compressed yield

![Villa with a swimming pool and landscaped garden in Marrakech](/images/articles/riad-appartement-villa-terrain-marrakech-2026-3.webp)

The villa attracts buyers for the lifestyle it offers, less for pure profitability: the long-term yield of a Palmeraie villa often falls below 4%, as the high acquisition price compresses the rent-to-price ratio. High-end short-term rental improves the gross yield (7 to 11% according to industry sources), but requires active management: pool, garden and security are ongoing costs, independent of occupancy rate.

A villa is almost always located in a subdivision governed by a charter and, where common areas exist, by the horizontal co-ownership rules of law no. 18-00. Our [dedicated analysis of villa investment in Marrakech](/articles/villa-marrakech-investissement) breaks down prices by neighborhood, real running costs and subdivision checks.

## Undeveloped land: the most patient investment

Buying land to build on yourself or to hold as a land reserve remains the cheapest option at purchase — 800 to 3,000 MAD/m² on the outskirts, against several tens of thousands of MAD/m² downtown — but also the one requiring the most upfront checks and the least liquid in the short term. Part of the peri-urban land around Marrakech remains under agricultural, collective (soulaliyate) or military (guich) status, three statuses that restrict or prohibit sale to a foreigner without a specific procedure.

For any land located outside the urban perimeter, a foreign buyer must obtain a certificate of non-agricultural vocation (AVNA), provided for under decree no. 2.04.683 and clarified by a joint circular from three ministerial departments dated 29 April 2022: without this certificate, the notary cannot process the sale. ## Which property for which investor profile

![Work table with documents and plans to compare a real estate investment project in Marrakech](/images/articles/riad-appartement-villa-terrain-marrakech-2026-4.webp)

| Main objective | Best-suited property type |
|---|---|
| Maximum rental yield, active management accepted | Riad to renovate, operated as a guesthouse |
| Legal simplicity and bank financing | Apartment in Guéliz or Agdal |
| Second home with supplementary rental income | Villa in the Palmeraie or Route de l'Ourika |
| Land reserve or custom-build project | Undeveloped land, legal status verified upfront |
| Limited entry budget, long horizon | Riad to renovate in the medina or peripheral land |

## Checklist before deciding

- Compare the price per **usable** m² (actual livable area), not just the m² price shown on the title deed
- Distinguish the advertised gross yield from the net yield after charges, vacancy and taxation
- Check the land title status (registered, moulkiya, collective, agricultural) before making any offer
- For land outside the urban perimeter bought by a foreigner, confirm that obtaining an AVNA is possible before signing a preliminary contract
- For an off-plan purchase, require the reservation contract and verify the legal escrow account exists
- Budget for management (concierge, syndic, upkeep) based on property type, not just the purchase price

## What to remember

There is no absolute "best" property type in Marrakech: the riad maximizes potential yield at the cost of heavier legal and operational complexity, the apartment secures financing and resale, the villa sells a lifestyle that pure profitability calculations do not always justify, and land rewards patience and upfront verification. The figures cited in this article are market averages observed by professionals in 2026; they must be confirmed property by property, with a local professional, before any financial commitment.
