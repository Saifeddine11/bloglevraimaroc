## Why the constituency matters

Marrakech includes Guéliz-Nakhil, Menara and Medina-Sidi Youssef Ben Ali. Casablanca has several constituencies, including Anfa, Aïn Chock and Ben M’Sick. Rabat-Océan and Rabat-Chellah are separate, as are Fez-North and Fez-South. A city-only list can assign a candidate to the wrong voter’s electoral area.

The cited party publications cover other constituencies, including Salé, Tétouan, Essaouira and southern provinces. PJD’s document also identifies regional list leaders. A regional nomination must not be presented as a local nomination in every city within that region.

## Endorsement, filing and acceptance

Endorsement is the party’s selection. Mandatory online declarations close at noon on 8 September. Original files must then be delivered at the appointment specified on the receipt, no later than noon on 9 September.

The Interior Ministry says local files go to the relevant provincial, prefectural or district-prefectural authority, while regional files go to the regional wilaya. Campaign photographs or personal social-media announcements do not substitute for these steps.

## What this tracker covers

Having two party lists here does not mean only two parties participate: the official portal reports 27. Further entries require the same documentation, with constituency, date and status. Absence from this selection is not evidence that someone is not standing.

To verify a change, look for a dated replacement announcement and the applicable administrative list. Keep the earlier announcement as history rather than silently combining versions.
