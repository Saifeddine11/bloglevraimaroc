## Italian Institutions Palace and city performances

The organiser names the Palais des Institutions Italiennes and activities across Tangier, including a free public stage at Bab Bhar. Its Experience page also mentions the Palais des Arts et de la Culture for an opening concert. Check the venue for your specific event: these are different places.

The overall dates cover 18–20 September, while the reviewed passes cover the 18th and 19th. We do not infer a third paid evening on the 20th without a matching programme and ticket. City events and further announcements require separate checks.

## Travelling to Tangier and choosing accommodation

Tanger-Ville is a railway arrival point, not the Italian Institutions Palace. Allow for a transfer and check the last train actually available if returning after a concert. Published finishing times are not yet detailed enough to guarantee a connection.
