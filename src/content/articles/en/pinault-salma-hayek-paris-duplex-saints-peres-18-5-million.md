## What is confirmed, and what remains attributed

At €37,000/sq m the price is high, yet below the absolute records published for some private Paris houses. Its value is comparative: a large Left Bank duplex can trade below asking while still retaining a very strong valuation.

The celebrity-linked names create visibility, but the real estate core lies in address, size, garden or garden-level configuration, building rarity and the depth of international buyers.

## The property in context

Architecture matters as much as price. The cited sources describe an asset whose value depends on rarity, privacy, specification level and the ability to convince a narrow pool of qualified buyers. At this level, comparison with a standard apartment or ordinary house makes little sense unless surface, land, address, constraints and possible use are corrected.

The right reflex is to treat every point as something to verify: surface, actual condition, price history, attached rights, costs, works, buyer confidentiality and publicly available evidence. Spectacular numbers attract attention, but they never summarize the quality of the file.

## Price, timeline and market signal

Price must be read with its timeline. A long-marketed asset, a price cut, an auction result and a private sale do not say the same thing. Time on market can signal limited demand, an ambitious initial ask, heavy works or simply the rarity of a buyer able to move fast.

In trophy property, asking price often works as a narrative starting point. The signed price, when confirmed, gives a stronger marker. Even then, one must know whether the surface includes or excludes gardens, annexes, outbuildings, furniture, special rights or future works.
