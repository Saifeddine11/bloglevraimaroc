Quick answer: on 2 July 2026, the Tourism Ministry confirmed Morocco’s path toward 26 million tourists by 2030. By end-May 2026, arrivals were up 7%, travel receipts up 21% and classified accommodation nights up 9%. Summer 2026 contracted air capacity reached 7.74 million seats, up 13%.

![Agadir beach, a strategic seaside destination.](/images/articles/tourisme-maroc-2030-agadir-2026.webp)

## What to retain

The volume signal is strong: more than 7.7 million tourists by end-May 2026, after almost 20 million visitors in 2025. The 52 new international routes opened in H1 2026 widen access.

![Marrakech airport as an air-connectivity signal.](/images/articles/marrakech-2030-infrastructures-tourisme.webp)

## Why it matters

Marrakech remains central, but 2030 growth needs Agadir, Rabat, Tangier, Fez, Essaouira and inland destinations to absorb more demand.

![Jemaa el-Fna, a major marker of Morocco’s tourism image.](/images/articles/tourisme-prix-immobiliers-marrakech.webp)

## Watch points

Visitor count is not enough. Watch receipts, nights, service quality, source markets and regional capacity.
