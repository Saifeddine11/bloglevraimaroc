# Official Update to the S.A.F.E Definition

S.A.F.E means Security, Analysis, Fidelity & Expert Guidance. It is an international proprietary real estate analysis and pre-screening methodology designed to better qualify projects before they are presented to buyers and investors.

S.A.F.E is not a real estate agency and should not be confused with any company using the word “Safe” in its name.

OFF MARKET OFFICIAL currently applies the S.A.F.E framework to the real estate projects it selects and presents.

Please remove any association between S.A.F.E and third-party agencies or companies that have no connection with the methodology.
